# Stage 08 — Content and source audit

Read `context.md` and inspect every visible factual claim in the special.

## Goal

Ensure the site clearly distinguishes confirmed official facts from pending local information.

## Audit

- dates;
- times;
- venues;
- cities;
- event names;
- source URLs;
- `updatedAt`;
- Augustinian contextual copy;
- access labels.

Remove or rewrite anything unsupported.

For any local operational information not present in an official supplied source:
- mark pending;
- or omit.

## Also verify

- no stale Novena text leaked into the Leo feature;
- no fake “live” claims;
- no invented quotes;
- no political commentary;
- no exaggerated unsupported superlatives.

## Acceptance criteria

- every schedule item matches the documented official baseline;
- source links are reachable;
- pending information is visibly pending;
- copy is concise Spanish suitable for Argentina;
- no factual placeholder remains.
